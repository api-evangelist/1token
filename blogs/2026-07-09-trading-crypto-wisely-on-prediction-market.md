Executive Summary The crypto derivatives landscape has undergone a profound structural shift. While centralized exchanges (CEXs) like Binance, OKX and Deribit continue to dominate institutional volume with perpetual swap, dated futures and vanilla options, decentralized and regulated prediction markets like Polymarket and Kalshi have emerged as powerful parallel trading venue.
